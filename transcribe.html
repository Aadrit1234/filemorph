Tips for Best Results
🎯
Clear Speech — Speak clearly and at a normal pace. Avoid mumbling.
🔇
Quiet Environment — Minimize background noise for better accuracy.
🌐
Internet Required — Web Speech API needs an internet connection for the speech model.
🇮🇳
Hindi Tips — Use standard Khadi Boli Hindi. Works best in Chrome/Edge. Also supports Marathi, Nepali, and other Devanagari languages.
🎤
Microphone — Use a good quality mic. Headset mics give better results than built-in laptop mics.
⏱️
Long Audio — For long files, the transcription auto-restarts. Just let it run — results appear in real-time.

Everything happens locally in your browser.
